# Win celebration presentation

Outside bonus features, evaluate the recorded gross return once at round end, including all cascades. Divide it by the actual charged wager, including any chance boost. No random celebration chance is used.

| Return / wager | Presentation | Existing illustration | Accent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Below 10× | Board highlights, cascade award, final return in controls; no full-screen ceremony | — | — |
| 10× to below 25× | Big win · The chalice overflows | Lesser Rite chalice | Amber |
| 25× to below 50× | Mega win · A tide of crimson | Blood Rite channels | Crimson |
| 50× to below 100× | Epic win · Crowned by the Hollow Halo | Greater Rite crown | Gold |
| 100× and above | Divine win · The entity bestows its favor | God Mode entity | Pale violet |

Thresholds are inclusive. Only the highest qualifying tier appears. Larger tiers have longer count-ups; all retain reveal, continue, skip, and automatic dismissal. Reduced motion removes the animated entrance. Replay uses the same recorded payout and cost. No-charge demonstrations use their mode's nominal wager for classification.

Bonus entry, retrigger, and final feature summaries retain their existing behavior, including zero-return summaries. This is a presentation change only: no probabilities, payouts, progression, or wallet rules change. Artwork reuses the existing Higgsfield illustrations; no new images were generated.
