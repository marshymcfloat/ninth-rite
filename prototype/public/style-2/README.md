# Style 2 · Engraved v1

Open `/` on the local URL printed by the prototype launcher. Old `/style-2` links redirect to the root.

The forty approved still exports are integrated under the isolated `media/engraved-v1/` namespace. Full-resolution artwork and production metadata remain in `prototype/art/style-2/engraved-v1/`.

## Presentation

- Mode-specific chapel, sanctuary, and God environments.
- Nine optically fitted symbols, modular frame, logo ornament, earned multiplier ladder, paired Blood and Offering states, and intervention textures.
- Four rite illustrations in selection, confirmation, and entrance screens.
- Dedicated feature-result tableau; separate Big, Mega, Epic, Divine, and maximum-return illustrations with live amount registration.
- Three mechanic illustrations available through Game help.
- Five registered engraved priest poses. Legacy videos remain on disk but are not loaded by this presentation.

`manifest.json` records the presentation version, frame slices, priest anchors, role mappings, and loading budget. `art-data.mjs` records export bounds and safe areas. `art.mjs` fits foreground assets uniformly while preserving transparent padding; weak registrations survive delayed symbol mounting and release discarded nodes.

## Typography

Cinzel is used for engraved headings and large values, Cinzel Decorative Bold for the logo, and Source Sans 3 for controls and body text. All files and their SIL Open Font License notices are local. Currency numerals retain readable lining/tabular forms. See `fonts/README.md` for provenance and glyph checks.

## Boundaries

No changes to rules, prices, payout thresholds, recorded books, or catalog selection. The ladder reflects existing earned Blood progression. It does not award an extra spin.

Existing recorded-event motion is retained. This integration adds no generated video, audio, paid generation, or publishing.
