     THE NINTH RITE

    The rites of the altar

    Raise the altar

    Winning clusters collect Blood. Fill five insets to reach the next earned multiplier on the ladder.

    Invoke the patron

    Three Offerings reveal a pending multiplier. Win the next tumble, or use the last chance on the following free spin.

    Enter the sanctuary

    Scatters unlock bonus rites. Three or more on an initial free-spin board award five more spins, except in God Mode.

    Groups of five or more matching symbols connected by shared edges pay. Winning symbols and disconnected symbols of the same type clear. New symbols tumble into place.

    5 winning clusters advance the altar. During bonuses, 3 Offerings invoke a pending multiplier. The invoking win uses the earned multiplier. The offer confirms before the next winning tumble pays. If the next tumble loses, the offer gets one last chance on the following free spin. A losing second chance expires it, even if more spins are awarded. No extra spin is granted to resolve an offer. Expiry keeps earned progress and surplus Offerings; spent Offerings are not refunded.

    Bonuses run their free spins automatically. In Lesser, Blood and Greater Rite, 3 or more Scatters on the initial board of any free spin award +5 free spins. This can happen repeatedly, including during awarded spins. Tumble refills do not retrigger. God Mode does not retrigger.
